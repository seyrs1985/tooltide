Frequently asked questions

How do you convert kilograms to stone?

Multiply the number of kilograms by 0.157473. For example, 10 kilograms = 1.57473 stone. The converter above does this for you and shows more decimal places than you will ever need.

How many stone are in one kilograms?

Exactly 0.157473 stone. This is a fixed definition, not an approximation, so the conversion is exact at any scale.

Is this converter accurate for professional use?

Yes — it uses full double-precision arithmetic and the official kilograms-to-stone factor, so results are reliable for schoolwork, cooking, fitness, travel and technical work.

Can I convert stone back to kilograms?

Yes. The converter is two-way: just type in the second box and the first box updates instantly with the reverse conversion.
