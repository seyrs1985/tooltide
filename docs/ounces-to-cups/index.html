Frequently asked questions

How do you convert fluid ounces to cups (US)?

Multiply the number of fluid ounces by 0.125. For example, 10 fluid ounces = 1.25 cups (US). The converter above does this for you and shows more decimal places than you will ever need.

How many cups (US) are in one fluid ounces?

Exactly 0.125 cups (US). This is a fixed definition, not an approximation, so the conversion is exact at any scale.
