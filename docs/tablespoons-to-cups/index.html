Frequently asked questions

How do you convert tablespoons to cups (US)?

Multiply the number of tablespoons by 0.0625. For example, 10 tablespoons = 0.625 cups (US). The converter above does this for you and shows more decimal places than you will ever need.

How many cups (US) are in one tablespoons?

Exactly 0.0625 cups (US). This is a fixed definition, not an approximation, so the conversion is exact at any scale.

Is this converter accurate for professional use?

Yes — it uses full double-precision arithmetic and the official tablespoons-to-cups (US) factor, so results are reliable for schoolwork, cooking, fitness, travel and technical work.

Can I convert cups (US) back to tablespoons?

Yes. The converter is two-way: just type in the second box and the first box updates instantly with the reverse conversion.
